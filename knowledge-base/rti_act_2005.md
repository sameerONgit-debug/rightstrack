# Right to Information Act 2005

## Overview
The Right to Information (RTI) Act 2005 is a fundamental legislation in India that empowers Indian citizens with the right to seek information from public authorities. It was enacted to promote transparency and accountability in governance.

## Chapter 1: Preliminary

### Section 1 - Short Title and Extent
- **Short Title**: Right to Information Act, 2005
- **Extent**: Extends to the whole of India except Jammu and Kashmir
- **Commencement**: October 12, 2005

### Section 2 - Definitions
- **"Information"**: Records held by public authorities that may be in any form (paper, electronic, video, etc.)
- **"Public authority"**: Any authority or body established or constituted by law or under the Government of India, State Government, or Local Body
- **"Right to information"**: Right to access information held by public authorities

## Chapter 2: Right to Information

### Section 3 - Right to Information
Every citizen shall have the right to request information from public authorities:
- The information must be held by or under the control of the public authority
- Citizens can request information in any form
- The public authority must provide information within the prescribed time limits

### Section 4 - Obligations of Public Authorities
Public authorities must:
- Maintain records properly
- Computerize records
- Publish information proactively
- Provide information to applicants
- Appoint Public Information Officers (PIOs)

### Section 5 - Proactive Disclosure
Every public authority shall make available to the public:
- Description of its organization, functions, and duties
- Decision-making procedures
- Norms for the discharge of functions
- Rules, regulations, instructions manuals
- Records held by it under the three categories mentioned in Section 4(1)(b)
- Particulars of any arrangement that exists for carrying out consultations

### Section 6 - Request for Access to Information

#### Section 6(1) - Application Procedure
A person may request information from any public authority by:
- Making a written application in English or in the official language of the area
- Specifying the particulars of the information sought
- Paying the prescribed fee
- Submitting through one of the prescribed modes (in writing or electronically)

#### Section 6(2) - Assistance to Applicants
If an applicant is unable to:
- Reduce the request to writing
- Pay the prescribed fee
- Is visually impaired

The Public Information Officer must provide reasonable assistance.

#### Section 6(3) - Applicant Information
The applicant does not have to give any reason for requesting information or any personal details except those that may be necessary to locate the information.

#### Section 6(4) - Language of Application
Application can be made in any language but:
- If made in a language other than English or the official language of the State, the public authority may request the applicant to resubmit in English or the official language
- This cannot be used to deny or delay information

#### Section 6(5) - Application Format
The application must:
- Include particulars of the information sought
- Be submitted in the prescribed format (Form A)
- Include proof of payment of the prescribed fee
- Be addressed to the Public Information Officer

## Chapter 3: Obligations of Public Information Officers

### Section 7 - Assistance to Applicant

#### Section 7(1) - Clarification of Request
If the application is not clear, the PIO should:
- Seek clarification from the applicant within 5 days
- Consider clarification received and provide information accordingly

#### Section 7(2) - Disposal Time
After receiving the application, the Public Information Officer shall:
- Make every effort to provide information within 30 days
- If the information contains any material relating to another public authority, that authority must be consulted

#### Section 7(3) - Extended Deadline
If providing the information:
- Would require searching through a large number of files
- Would impose substantial burden
- Would divert the resources of the public authority

The deadline may be extended by an additional 20 days with written notice to the applicant stating reasons for extension.

#### Section 7(4) - Transfer of Application
If the application cannot be replied to by the concerned public authority:
- The application shall be transferred to the concerned public authority within 5 days
- The applicant shall be informed about the transfer

### Section 8 - Exemptions

#### Section 8(1) - Grounds for Exemption
A public authority may refuse to give information which would:
- Prejudice the sovereignty and integrity of India
- Disclose foreign relations information
- Affect the security or economic interests of the State
- Prejudice the detection, investigation or prosecution of offences or the apprehension of offenders
- Disclose information received in confidence from foreign governments
- Disclose information the disclosure of which would cause harm to wildlife, forestry or environment
- Prejudice the security of any building or installation
- Disclose information which would impede the process of investigation or apprehension or prosecution of offenders

#### Section 8(2) - Security Classified Information
Information related to security of State cannot be disclosed if classified as confidential under the Official Secrets Act, 1923, except:
- After appropriate declassification
- By the competent authority

### Section 9 - Exemptions Contd.

#### Section 9(1) - Personal Information
Information which relates to personal information:
- Subject to exceptions for information relating to persons acting in official capacity whose disclosure is necessary for public interest
- Also exempted: Information relating to informants and sources of information

#### Section 9(2) - Partial Disclosure
If only part of the information can be provided:
- Provide the information that is not exempt
- Give reasons for the exemption
- Offer the applicant to prefer an appeal

## Chapter 4: Appeal

### Section 19 - First Appeal

#### Section 19(1) - Right of Appeal
If an applicant is not satisfied with:
- The decision of the Public Information Officer
- The manner in which information is provided
- The refusal to provide information
- The extension of time under Section 7(3)

The applicant may appeal to:
- The designated senior officer or the authority (not subordinate to the PIO) within 30 days of receiving the decision

#### Section 19(2) - Appeal Procedure
On receipt of the appeal:
- The designated officer shall, if possible, pass orders within 30 days
- In circumstances where large amount of information is involved, may extend by 20 days
- The officer shall:
  - Confirm, modify, or reject the decision
  - Make recommendations
  - Remit the decision back to the Public Information Officer

### Section 19(3) - Hearing
The officer may:
- Hear the applicant orally if the applicant so desires
- Examine the information sought by the applicant

## Chapter 5: Central Information Commission and State Information Commissions

### Section 12 - Constitution of Central Information Commission
The Central Government shall constitute a Central Information Commission

### Section 12(1) - Composition
The Central Information Commission shall consist of:
- Chief Information Commissioner (CIC)
- Not more than two Information Commissioners

### Section 12(4) - Qualification
The CIC and Information Commissioners shall:
- Have been or be a distinguished person with impeccable integrity
- Have wide knowledge and experience in
  - Law
  - Science and technology
  - Social and economic matters
  - Administration and governance
- Not have been convicted of any offence involving moral turpitude

### Section 13 - Constitution of State Information Commissions
Each State shall constitute:
- One State Information Commission
- With a Chief Information Commissioner and number of Information Commissioners as prescribed

## Chapter 6: Second Appeal

### Section 20 - Second Appeal (Appeal to Central Information Commission)

If an applicant is not satisfied with the order passed by the:
- Designated officer under Section 19
- State Information Commission

The applicant may file a second appeal to:
- The Central Information Commission
- Within 90 days from the date of receiving the order

### Section 20(1) - Appeal to Central Information Commission
The Central Information Commission may:
- Confirm, modify, or reject the decision
- Issue directions to the public authority to provide information
- Award reasonable compensation to the applicant for delay or non-compliance

## Procedural Guidelines

### Filing RTI Application - Step-by-Step
1. **Identify the correct public authority** - Know which authority holds the information
2. **Prepare the application** - Write clear, specific request in writing
3. **Submit with prescribed fee** - Obtain Form A, fill it, include fee (₹10 or as per State rules)
4. **Track the application** - Note the receipt/reference number
5. **Monitor response timeline** - RTI reply is mandated within 30 days
6. **File First Appeal** (if required) - Within 30 days at designated officer
7. **File Second Appeal** (if required) - Within 90 days at State/Central Information Commission

### Limitations on RTI Requests
- **Personal Information**: Cannot seek information about others without justification
- **Large Information**: Cannot request thousands of pages without specificity
- **Frivolous Requests**: Public authorities can refuse vexatious applications
- **Classified Information**: Cannot seek information affecting national security

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- **Section 20(2)**: Information Commission may award compensation up to ₹25,000
- **Section 23**: PIO acting in bad faith liable for action
- **Section 24**: Authority not complying with orders can face action

## Common Issues and Resolutions

### Issue: PIO did not respond within 30 days
**Resolution**: File First Appeal within 30 days claiming default

### Issue: Information provided is incomplete or not relevant
**Resolution**: File First Appeal specifically stating how information is incomplete

### Issue: Request rejected under exemption clause
**Resolution**: File First Appeal challenging the exemption grounds with reasoning

### Issue: Unsatisfied with First Appeal decision
**Resolution**: File Second Appeal to State Information Commission within 90 days
