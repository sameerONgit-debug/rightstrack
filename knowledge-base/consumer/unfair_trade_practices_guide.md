# Consumer Protection Act 2019 — Practical Grievance Redressal Guide

## Core Actionable Grounds for Consumers
1. **Deficiency in Service (Section 2(11))**: Non-delivery, delayed service, refusal to fulfill warranty, defective repair, or failure to honor contract terms.
2. **Defective Goods (Section 2(10))**: Product fails to perform as advertised, dangerous defects, or manufacturing flaws.
3. **Unfair Trade Practice (Section 2(47))**: Misleading advertisements, refusing refunds after cancellation within policy, bait-and-switch tactics, or non-issuance of tax invoice.

## Escalation Flow
1. **Pre-Litigation Legal Notice**: Formal written communication giving 15 to 30 days to resolve.
2. **E-Daakhil / District Consumer Commission (DCDRC)**: Filing under Section 35.
3. **Statutory Timeline (Section 38(2)(a))**: Opposite party must respond within 30 days (+15 days discretionary extension).
