# Standard RTI Application Templates & Guidance

## Structure of a Standard RTI Application (Section 6(1))
1. **Public Authority Address**: Specific Central/State Public Information Officer (CPIO/SPIO).
2. **Applicant Identity & Contact**: Name, full address, email/phone.
3. **Specific Point-Wise Queries**: Concise, numbered questions asking for factual records, tender documents, inspection notes, or order copies.
4. **Statutory Reference**: Direct invocation of Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act 2005.
5. **Fee Payment**: Mention of statutory Rs. 10 fee (court fee stamp, postal order, or online payment receipt).

## Deemed Refusal & First Appeal Protocol (Section 19(1))
- When the 30-day timeline mandated under Section 7(1) expires with zero reply, Section 7(2) triggers a statutory presumption of "Deemed Refusal".
- The citizen is immediately entitled to file a First Appeal within 30 days before the First Appellate Authority (FAA) under Section 19(1).
- No further application fee is required for filing the First Appeal.
